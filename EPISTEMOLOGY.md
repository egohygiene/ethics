# How Ethics knows what it says

Understand the system. Examine the evidence. Preserve human dignity. Improve what we can.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Observed fact | A directly documented event or record, within its stated boundary |
| Documented estimate | A source's modeled or estimated quantity, not direct universal measurement |
| Projection | A conditional future scenario, not an outcome |
| Interpretation | Reasoning from evidence, with alternatives and limitations |
| Author's perspective | Personal values or experience, not empirical proof |
| Open question | An unresolved question with a known evidence gap |

A source can be authoritative about its own method and still be incomplete. Institutions' claims about themselves are not independent verification. Conflicting records remain visible. Recency alone does not resolve a conflict.

Evidence precedes interpretation. Each chart is an inspectable transformation of locatable source records. A successful link check is not verification of a claim. An agent's source comparison is labeled agent_checked; human review stays pending. We expose limitations even when they weaken the author's preferred position.

## Intellectual commitments

Mathematical methods, companies deploying them, governance choices, and individual uses are different objects of ethical analysis. Harms involving consent, labor, copyright, safety, water, or electricity deserve serious scrutiny. Benefits require suitable evidence too. Efficiency does not imply declining total demand. Choosing traditional craft or declining AI is compatible with creative freedom for others.

We do not infer motives, label criticism propaganda, rank moral worth, claim AI has saved more lives than it has harmed, or mistake the workflow-efficiency hypothesis for measured results. Manual craft, authorship, consent, and artistic labor have independent value.

## Corrections

Open an evidence issue with the affected record ID, primary source, locator, and proposed correction. Preserve superseded context in Git. Quantitative corrections require validation and source comparison; a material change returns to pending human review. No silent rewriting of source evidence to match a narrative.
